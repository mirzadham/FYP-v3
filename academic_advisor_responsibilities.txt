Responsibilities of the Academic Advisor

1.1 Curriculum & Systems Guidance: Assist students in understanding the curriculum, semester system, registration system, examination system, and credit review required to complete their studies.


1.2 Consultation: Dedicate time to discuss studies, career paths, and other matters by making prior appointments with the student.


1.3 Performance Monitoring: Monitor performance progress online (via SMP) and advise students accordingly.


1.4 Referrals for Special Attention: Identify and refer students requiring special attention, consultation, or guidance to an instructor, university counselor, or appropriate officer.


1.5 Positive Guidance: Provide advice and guidance to students in a positive manner, fostering a sense of appreciation among students while opening opportunities for them to achieve their respective goals.


1.6 Record Keeping: Record every meeting and discussion held.


1.7 Non-Academic Support: Assist students in resolving non-academic problems and, if necessary, refer the matter to the relevant parties.


1.8 Activity Monitoring: Monitor activities involving all students under their advisement.


1.9 Registration Activation: Activate course registration for supervised students after the student discusses it with the Academic Advisor (PA) via SMP during the course registration week.


1.10 Registration Confirmation: Validate/Confirm student course registration in the SMP-Course Registration Module.
